CHAPTER 1: UNDERSTANDING CAD ARCHITECTURE & SYSTEM STATS

Every CAD assigned by the Mind—humanity’s primary governing Artificial Intelligence—bonds directly with the user's neural network and cellular structure. Unlike standard military hardware, a Combat Assistance Device evolves based on combat data, neurological strain, and tactical adaptation.

CADs evaluate user capabilities using seven core attribute metrics:

1. Strength (STR): Measures physical force generation, kinetic impact capacity, and armor-piercing muscle output.
2. Speed (SPD): Dictates movement velocity, acceleration burst, and spatial relocation speed during engagement.
3. Endurance (END): Regulates stamina depletion, shock absorption, and physical fatigue mitigation under high thermal strain.
4. Cognition (COG): Controls combat processing speed, reaction timing, optical tracking, and tactical calculation.
5. Offense (OFF): Evaluates weapon lethality, energy blade manifestation density, and strike precision.
6. Defense (DEF): Determines dynamic energy shielding strength, armor plate durability, and kinetic deflection efficiency.
7. Growth (GRW): The rarest and most critical attribute. Growth determines the rate at which all other stats upgrade through combat experience and physical strain.

Stat ranks progress through standard tiers: F0 through F9, E0 through E9, D0 through D9, C0 through C9, B0 through B9, A0 through A9, and the legendary S-Tier. While most baseline cadets manifest with initial stats between F4 and E2, rare anomalies occur under special AI assignments.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHAPTER 2: CAD CLASSIFICATIONS AND WEAPON TYPES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Upon initial assignment, a CAD manifests into one of six standard combat classes, or a rare seventh anomaly class:

- Saber: Balanced combatants utilizing medium-length blades. High adaptability, balanced Strength and Speed.
- Brawler: Close-quarters specialists using augmented gauntlets and greaves. Relies on maximum Speed, high Cognition, and rapid strike combinations.
- Lancer: Medium-to-long reach specialists equipped with polearms. Excellent thrusting power and tactical zone control.
- Duelist: Precision speed-fighters wielding twin light blades or rapiers. Prioritizes extreme Speed and Cognition over raw Defense.
- Phalanx: Heavy defense specialists equipped with massive tower shields and short swords. High Endurance and Defense, designed to hold frontlines against heavy ordinance.
- Mauler: Devastating heavy-strike specialists wielding massive two-handed hammers or axes. High Strength and Offense with low baseline Speed.
- A-Type (Atypical): Unpredictable manifestation class. A-Types do not conform to standard weaponry and often manifest unique devices, variable armor forms, or abnormal stat scaling metrics.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHAPTER 3: ACADEMY TOURNAMENT PROTOCOLS & ARENA RULES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

At the Galens Institute, combat capabilities are tested through sanctioned intra-academy sparring matches and official tournament circuits.

1. Arena Callouts: Cadets assemble in designated combat zones. When summoned, CADs undergo "Call"—manifesting physical armor plating and energy weaponry from the user's reactive band.
2. Reactive Shielding & Safety Field: During official academy duels, weapons interact with localized forcefields generated by the CADs. When a strike delivers enough simulated lethal force to breach a cadet's defensive buffer, the arena system registers a "Fatal Blow" and automatically freezes combat to prevent actual mortality.
3. Rank Ascension: Cadets earn ranking points based on match performance, tactical ingenuity, and stat evolution. Rank standings dictate access to high-tier gravity training rooms, advanced sparring simulators, and selection for inter-system military tournament representation.

CHAPTER 4: FIELD COMBAT LOG - REIDON WARD VS. LOGAN GRANT (SPARRING MATCH #104)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Location: Galens Primary Training Arena 3
Class: Year 1 Placement Sparring

Match Summary:
Cadet Reidon Ward (A-Type) engaged Cadet Logan Grant (Mauler). At match start, Grant immediately asserted spatial dominance utilizing his massive warhammer, leveraging an overwhelming Strength stat (D4) against Ward's initial low-tier baseline specs (F3 Strength, F2 Defense).

Ward relied on hyper-efficient Cognition and rapid footwork to evade initial heavy kinetic sweeps. By calculating Grant's recovery frames after heavy swings, Ward closed distance to land precise strike sequences on vulnerable armor joints. Although Ward sustained significant kinetic impact damage from partial shield clips, his CAD demonstrated an abnormal Growth acceleration burst mid-combat. 

Result:
Grant secured match victory via shield collapse at 4 minutes, 12 seconds. However, post-match diagnostics indicated Ward's CAD experienced an unprecedented stat advancement step during the duel, confirming the S-Tier Growth rating assigned by the Collective AI.
